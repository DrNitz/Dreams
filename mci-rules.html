The Complete Regulatory Timeline

How India's foreign medical graduate rules evolved from 2002 to 2026. Know which rules apply to YOU based on when you joined.

2002 — Screening Test Regulations Introduced
MCI made FMGE (Foreign Medical Graduate Examination) mandatory for all Indian citizens with foreign medical degrees. The exam was originally 3 separate papers. Maximum 3 attempts were allowed (later removed in 2003, making attempts unlimited).
Applies to all pre-2021 students
2010 — Same Institution Rule Added
Gazette notification dated 16 April 2010 made it mandatory that students must complete their entire medical course from the SAME foreign institution. No transfers between universities allowed. This was a Supreme Court-directed change.
Critical for Philippines students
2018 — NEET Becomes Mandatory for Abroad Admission
Gazette notification dated 01 March 2018: Indian citizens/OCI intending to obtain primary medical qualification from any foreign institution on or after May 2018 must mandatorily qualify NEET-UG. NEET result is treated as the Eligibility Certificate.
No NEET = No FMGE eligibility
2021 — NMC Replaces MCI; FMGL Regulations Gazette (18 Nov 2021)
The National Medical Commission (Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate) Regulations, 2021 replaced the old screening test framework. Key new rules: 54-month minimum course duration, 12-month internship abroad, English medium mandatory, same institution rule reinforced, and NExT exam introduced as replacement for FMGE.
Applies to students joining on/after 18 Nov 2021
2023 — NExT Regulations & 10-Year Cap Introduced
NMC National Exit Test Regulations, 2023 (27 June 2023) stated that candidates must pass both NExT Step-1 and Step-2 within 10 years of joining the MBBS course. Combined with CRMI rules, this created a very tight timeline for foreign graduates. Implementation was later deferred.
Implementation deferred — FMGE continues
2026 — NMC Clarifications & FMGE Continues
As of 2026, FMGE remains the active screening test (NExT deferred). NMC issued clarifications in March 2026 tightening university recognition criteria. Philippines degrees require case-by-case verification. NBE FMGE June 2026 bulletin confirms zero tolerance for false admission date claims.
Current active framework

NMC FMGL Regulations 2021 — The 7 Rules That Matter

If you joined your foreign medical college on or after 18 November 2021, these 7 rules are non-negotiable. Break even one, and your degree is invalid for Indian practice.

Which rules apply to you? If you joined BEFORE 18 Nov 2021, Old 2002 Screening Test Regulations apply. If you joined ON or AFTER 18 Nov 2021, FMGL 2021 applies. You cannot choose which rules to follow.

154-Month Minimum Course Duration

Your foreign medical degree course must have a minimum duration of 54 months (4.5 years) of academic study. This does NOT include the internship year. For Philippines, this means your MD program itself must be at least 4.5 years long. The old 4-year MD programs no longer qualify under FMGL 2021.

Philippines reality: Most NMC-approved Philippines colleges now offer 54-month MD programs (or BS+MD combined). Always verify the exact duration in your admission letter.

212-Month Internship Abroad (Same University)

You must complete a 12-month internship/clinical clerkship at the SAME university where you did your academic course. The internship must be hospital-based with hands-on patient exposure. Online or virtual internships are NOT accepted.

Philippines reality: All 6 colleges I recommend (GCM Gullas, SWU, Perpetual, DMSF, Brokenshire, JMC) offer 12-month internships at their affiliated hospitals. Confirm this in writing before paying fees.

3English Medium Mandatory

The entire course — lectures, exams, clinical training, and internship — must be in English. Bilingual programs or programs with local-language clinical rotations do NOT qualify. NMC now cross-verifies medium-of-instruction certificates during FMGE eligibility checks.

Philippines advantage: Philippines is the largest English-speaking country in Asia. All 6 recommended colleges teach entirely in English. This is a massive advantage over Russia or China.

4Same Institution Rule

You must complete your entire academic course AND internship from the same university in the same country. You cannot split your course between two universities or do your internship at a different institution. Rotations in "partner" hospitals under different administration are risky.

Red flag: If an agency tells you that you can do your internship in India or at a "partner hospital" in another city — that is NON-COMPLIANT under FMGL 2021.

5NEET-UG Mandatory Before Admission

Indian citizens must qualify NEET-UG before taking admission to any foreign medical institution. The NEET result is valid for 3 years and serves as your Eligibility Certificate. No NEET = No FMGE/NExT eligibility = No practice in India.

Philippines BSBio route: Students who did NOT qualify NEET can enroll in BSBio (pre-med) in Philippines, prepare for NEET while studying, and transition to MD after clearing NEET. This is 100% legal because BSBio is NOT the medical degree — MD is.

6Course Must Be Completed Within 10 Years

The total duration of your foreign medical graduation course (academic + internship) must be completed within 10 years from the date of joining. This includes any gaps, backlogs, or repeated years.

Warning: The NExT 2023 regulations initially suggested passing both Step-1 and Step-2 within 10 years of joining, which would be extremely tight for foreign graduates. This was deferred, but the 10-year course completion rule remains active.

7FMGE/NExT + CRMI in India

After your foreign degree, you must:

  • Pass FMGE (currently active) or NExT Step-1 (when implemented)
  • Complete 12-month Compulsory Rotating Medical Internship (CRMI) in an NMC-approved Indian hospital
  • Only then can you register with State Medical Council and practice

CRMI seat limit: Medical colleges can allocate only up to 7.5% of their intern quota to foreign medical graduates. This means FMGs often face delays getting internship seats.

FMGE Eligibility — Do You Qualify?

Exact criteria from the NBE June 2026 bulletin. Check every point before you plan your return to India.

Mandatory Requirements to Appear for FMGE

  • Citizenship: You must be an Indian citizen or Overseas Citizen of India (OCI). Foreign nationals cannot appear.
  • Primary Medical Qualification: You must hold a primary medical qualification (MBBS/MD equivalent) from a recognized foreign university.
  • Embassy Verification: Your degree must be confirmed by the Indian Embassy in that country as a recognized qualification for medical practice there.
  • NEET Qualification: If you took admission abroad on or after May 2018, you MUST have qualified NEET-UG. No exceptions.
  • Internship Completion: You must have completed your 12-month internship at the same foreign university (if applicable to your course).
  • Eligibility Certificate: For pre-2018 students, an Eligibility Certificate from MCI/NMC was required. For post-2018 students, NEET result serves as this certificate.

NBE Warning (June 2026 Bulletin): "Any instances of furnishing incorrect information/documents detected during any stage of the examination process and/or subsequently while/after seeking registration with a medical council will automatically disqualify the candidate... Further, if such instances go undetected during any verification process but are detected in subsequent years, such disqualification will take place with retrospective effect." Never fake your admission date.

Frequently Asked Questions — MCI/NMC Rules

Straight answers about regulations, eligibility, and what agencies will not tell you.

I joined before 18 Nov 2021. Do the new FMGL rules apply to me?
No. According to the NMC Guidelines for Registration of Foreign Medical Graduates (2022), students who joined their foreign medical education prior to 18 November 2021 are governed by the old Screening Test Regulations, 2002. The FMGL 2021 rules do NOT apply retrospectively. However, you must still clear FMGE and complete CRMI in India.
Is the Philippines MD degree valid in India?
Yes — if from an NMC-recognized college. The Philippines MD is equivalent to India's MBBS. After your MD, you must clear FMGE/NExT and complete 12-month CRMI in India. All 6 colleges I recommend (GCM Gullas, SWU, Perpetual, DMSF, Brokenshire, JMC) are compliant. Always verify on WDOMS and the NMC website.
Can I do my internship in India instead of Philippines?
No — not under FMGL 2021. The 12-month internship MUST be completed at the same foreign university where you did your academic course. After that, you do a SECOND 12-month internship (CRMI) in India. So yes, you will do internships in both countries — but the foreign one is mandatory first. Agencies promising "internship in India" to save costs are misleading you.
What happens if my college is not WDOMS listed?
Your degree will be invalid for Indian practice. WDOMS (World Directory of Medical Schools) listing is the first filter NMC uses. If your college is not on WDOMS, you cannot appear for FMGE, cannot register with any State Medical Council, and cannot practice in India. Verify BEFORE paying fees: Go to search.wdoms.org and search your exact university name.
Do I need to clear NEET before joining BSBio in Philippines?
No. BSBio (Bachelor of Science in Biology) is a pre-medical course, NOT the medical degree. NEET is mandatory only before joining the MD program. Many students enroll in BSBio without NEET, prepare for NEET while in Philippines, and transition to MD after qualifying. This is 100% legal and NMC-compliant because your "admission" to the foreign medical course (MD) happens after NEET, not the BSBio.
Will NExT replace FMGE? When?
NExT (National Exit Test) was announced to replace FMGE, but its implementation has been deferred multiple times. As of 2026, FMGE remains the active screening test for foreign medical graduates. When NExT is implemented, it will be a common exit exam for both Indian and foreign graduates. My advice: do not wait for NExT. Start preparing for FMGE from Year 1. The syllabus overlap is nearly 100%, so your preparation will not be wasted.
Can an agency guarantee my FMGE eligibility?
No agency can guarantee eligibility. Only NMC and NBE determine your eligibility based on your documents, university recognition, and compliance with regulations. Agencies that claim "100% eligibility guarantee" or "special NMC connections" are lying. The only way to ensure eligibility is to verify every rule yourself before admission. What is the 7.5% CRMI seat cap? How does it affect me?
Under NMC CRMI Regulations 2021, Indian medical colleges can allocate only up to 7.5% of their total intern seats to foreign medical graduates. This means after clearing FMGE, you may face a waiting period before getting an internship seat in India. The allocation is usually merit-based or through counseling. Start your FMGE preparation early so you can clear it quickly and secure your place in the internship queue.
