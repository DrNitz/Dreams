NMC Public Notice March 2026 — New Rules Every FMG Must Know

Notification No. U-15024/15/2024-UGMEB(Pt) dated 05 March 2026. This is the most important NMC update for foreign medical students in 2026. Read the exact clarifications below.

Breaking News: NMC issued Public Notice on 05 March 2026 (signed by Director UGMEB Ram Pratap) clarifying that online medical education cannot be compensated without genuinely extending the study period. Fake compensation certificates will lead to permanent disqualification from FMGE and Indian medical practice. This affects ALL foreign medical graduates who studied online during COVID-19.

1CRMI Applicability — Which Rules Apply to You?

The March 2026 notification makes it crystal clear:

  • Joined BEFORE 18 November 2021: You are governed by the old Screening Test Regulations 2002. If you have duly compensated online studies with physical onsite clinical/theory training AND completed 1-year internship at your foreign university AND passed exams equivalent to MBBS — you follow the old rules.
  • Joined AFTER 18 November 2021: You are governed by FMGL Regulations 2021 + CRMI Regulations 2021. You MUST undergo 1-year mandatory internship in India (CRMI) after your foreign degree. All other conditions from the 7 December 2023 notice remain in force.

"The students who had taken admission on or before 18.11.2021, the date of notification of the Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations, 2021 (FMGL, 2021), and who have duly compensated the period of online instruction by undergoing physical onsite clinical and/or theory training, together with one year of internship in the concerned foreign medical college or university, and who have qualified the examinations equivalent to the MBBS examination in India, shall be considered in accordance with the provisions of the Screening Test Regulations."

— NMC Public Notice U-15024/15/2024-UGMEB(Pt), 05 March 2026

You cannot choose which rules to follow. Your admission date decides everything.

2Online Study CANNOT Be Compensated Without Extending Study Period

This is the most critical clarification in the 2026 notification:

  • Compensation without extension of the study period is NOT acceptable. It is contrary to FMGL Regulations 2021.
  • The course and clerkship must commensurate with the Indian MBBS course (Graduate Medical Education Regulations 1997).
  • Medical education requires substantial hands-on learning, clinical exposure, and practical experience — which cannot be effectively substituted by virtual instruction alone.
  • Any FMG who studied a portion of their course online must undergo equivalent in-person training to make up for that exact duration.

"Compensation without extension of the study period is not acceptable, as it is contrary to FMGL Regulations, 2021, which require the course and clerkship to be commensurate with the MBBS course in India... foreign medical institutions that issue a 'compensation certificate' without actually extending the period of study and without ensuring that the additional training has occurred in a physical mode are acting in contradiction to the intent and requirements of the FMGL Regulations. Such certificates, issued without genuine compensation and extension of study period, undermine the very spirit and purpose of the FMGL Regulations, 2021."

— NMC Public Notice U-15024/15/2024-UGMEB(Pt), 05 March 2026

Red flag: If your university issued a "compensation certificate" without actually extending your study period and without ensuring additional training occurred in physical mode — that certificate is INVALID. NMC will reject your registration.

3Compensation Certificate Must Contain Extended Academic Period Details

The March 2026 notification mandates that compensation certificates MUST specify:

  • Details of subjects completed during offline compensation (both theory and practical)
  • Clinical subjects undertaken during physical onsite compensation
  • Mandatory clerkship details completed during the compensation period
  • Extended academic period — the exact duration added to compensate for online study

"It is imperative to verify the authenticity of the claim of Foreign Medical Graduates (FMGs) regarding the offline compensation for the remaining duration of the medical course, which must include both theoretical and practical components. Accordingly, the compensation certificate issued by the concerned foreign medical institution must clearly specify the details of the subjects and the duration completed by the FMG, i.e., The certificate must specify theory, clinical subjects, and mandatory clerkship undertaken during offline compensation, etc."

— NMC Public Notice U-15024/15/2024-UGMEB(Pt), 05 March 2026

Additionally, the transcript of record must be duly apostilled and authenticated by the Indian Embassy or High Commission in the respective country.

4Two Academic Years CANNOT Be Compressed Into One Year

NMC explicitly clarifies that academic years cannot be compressed. The entire online study period must be adequately compensated through physical onsite training. You cannot complete a 2-year curriculum in 1 year of offline study.

"Whether the curriculum for the two academic years can be completed in one year of offline study? No. As clarified at Sl. No. 1, academic years cannot be compressed, and the entire online study period must be adequately compensated through physical onsite training."

— NMC Public Notice U-15024/15/2024-UGMEB(Pt), 05 March 2026

This directly affects students who did online classes during COVID-19 and are now trying to rush through compensation. Slow down and do it right. Your entire career depends on this.

5State Medical Councils Have Primary Responsibility for FMG Registration

The March 2026 notification places the primary responsibility for processing FMG applications on State Medical Councils (SMCs), in consultation with State Directorates of Medical Education (DMEs).

SMCs must verify and ensure:

  • The FMG has completed compensatory academics (theory + practical) at the same foreign medical institution
  • Verification through visa stamping and entry/exit records in the passport
  • The FMG has qualified the Screening Test/FMGL Examination
  • The FMG has completed 1-year CRMI in India as prescribed

"The primary responsibility for processing applications of Foreign Medical Graduates (FMGs)... rests exclusively with the respective State Medical Councils (SMCs), in consultation with the concerned State Directorate of Medical Education (DME)."

— NMC Public Notice U-15024/15/2024-UGMEB(Pt), 05 March 2026

Prescribed sequence: (i) Qualify Screening Test + submit degree/clerkship certificates → (ii) Grant of provisional registration for CRMI → (iii) Grant of permanent registration after CRMI completion.

Warning: No SMC shall grant permanent registration unless ALL conditions are fully verified. Any registration granted without following these rules will be considered a regulatory violation and can be revoked retrospectively.

Philippines students: If any portion of your course was online (especially during 2020–2022), immediately contact your university to get a proper compensation certificate with extended study period details. Get your transcript apostilled and authenticated by the Indian Embassy in Manila. Do not wait until after graduation.

NMC FMGL Regulations 2021 — 7 Rules You Cannot Ignore

The foundation of all current NMC rules for MBBS abroad. Violate any one and your degree is worthless in India.

154-Month Minimum Course Duration

The foreign medical degree must be at least 54 months (4.5 years) of academic study, excluding internship. The course must commensurate with the Indian MBBS curriculum under Graduate Medical Education Regulations 1997.

Philippines: Old 4-year MD programs no longer qualify. Ensure your college offers 54-month MD or integrated BS-MD pathway.

212-Month Internship at Same University

You must complete 12 months of internship/clerkship at the same foreign medical institution where you did your academic course. Internship at a different hospital or in India is NOT allowed under FMGL 2021.

3NEET Mandatory Before Admission

You must qualify NEET-UG before joining the foreign medical course (MD/MBBS equivalent). BSBio is exempt since it is pre-medical, not the medical degree itself.

4English Medium of Instruction

The entire course must be taught in English medium. You must obtain an official English medium certificate from your university.

5WDOMS Listing Mandatory

Your university MUST be listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS). Verify at search.wdoms.org BEFORE paying any fees.

6Same Institution Rule

Your academic course AND internship must be at the same institution. Transfers between universities are generally not permitted unless under specific NMC-approved schemes (like Ukraine Academic Mobility).

7Eligibility Certificate from NMC

Post-2021 students should obtain an Eligibility Certificate from NMC before joining the foreign medical course. This proves NEET qualification and basic eligibility.

Frequently Asked Questions — NMC Rules 2026

Quick answers to the most searched questions about NMC notification 2026, Philippines MBBS, FMGE, and CRMI.

What is the new NMC notification March 2026 for foreign medical graduates?
The NMC Public Notice dated 05 March 2026 (U-15024/15/2024-UGMEB) clarifies mandatory physical onsite compensation classes for online studies undertaken by foreign medical students. Key points: (1) Students who joined before 18.11.2021 with compensated online studies are governed by old Screening Test Regulations; (2) Students who joined after 18.11.2021 are governed by CRMI Regulations 2021 and need 1 year mandatory internship in India; (3) Online study CANNOT be compensated without extending the study period; (4) Compensation certificates must specify extended academic period details; (5) Two academic years CANNOT be compressed into one year.
Where can I download the NMC March 2026 notification PDF?

The PDF contains all 5 clarifications regarding CRMI applicability, online study compensation, compensation certificate requirements, academic year compression, and State Medical Council responsibilities.
Is there a new Philippines MCI ban in 2026?
No, there is no ban on Philippines MBBS in 2026. However, NMC has tightened verification requirements. The March 2026 notification emphasizes that degrees from Philippines colleges must be verified through WDOMS, and students must have completed 54-month academic courses with 12-month onsite internship at the same university. Students from non-WDOMS listed colleges or with inadequate clinical training face registration rejection. All 6 NMC-approved colleges recommended on drnitz.com remain valid.
What are the NMC rules for 54 months course duration?
Under NMC FMGL Regulations 2021, the foreign medical degree course must have a minimum duration of 54 months (4.5 years) of academic study, excluding the internship year. The course and internship must commensurate with the Indian MBBS curriculum. Old 4-year MD programs no longer qualify. Philippines colleges now offer 54-month MD programs or integrated BS+MD pathways that meet this requirement.
What is the compensation certificate rule under NMC 2026?
The March 2026 NMC notification states that any portion of medical education undertaken online (particularly during COVID-19) must be compensated through equivalent in-person physical onsite training. The compensation certificate issued by the foreign university must clearly specify: (1) Details of subjects completed during offline compensation, (2) Theory and clinical subjects undertaken, (3) Mandatory clerkship details, (4) Extended academic period. Certificates issued without genuine compensation and extension of study period are invalid and will lead to disqualification.
What did NMC say about online study compensation without extending the study period?
The NMC March 2026 notification explicitly states that compensation without extension of the study period is NOT acceptable as it is contrary to FMGL Regulations 2021. Medical education requires substantial hands-on learning, clinical exposure, and practical experience which cannot be substituted by virtual instruction alone. Foreign medical institutions issuing compensation certificates without actually extending the study period and without ensuring additional training occurred in physical mode are acting in contradiction to FMGL Regulations. Such certificates are invalid.
I joined before 18 Nov 2021. Do the new FMGL rules apply to me?
No. According to the NMC March 2026 notification, students who joined their foreign medical education prior to 18 November 2021, who have duly compensated online studies with physical onsite training and completed one year internship at the foreign university, are governed by the old Screening Test Regulations 2002. Students who joined after 18.11.2021 are governed by FMGL 2021 and CRMI Regulations 2021, requiring one year mandatory internship in India.
Is the Philippines MD degree valid in India in 2026?
Yes — if from an NMC-recognized and WDOMS-listed college. The Philippines MD is equivalent to India's MBBS. After MD, you must clear FMGE and complete 12-month CRMI in India. The March 2026 notification requires additional verification of onsite compensation for any online training. All 6 colleges recommended on drnitz.com (GCM Gullas, SWU, Perpetual, DMSF, Brokenshire, JMC) are compliant. Always verify on search.wdoms.org before admission.
What is the 7.5% CRMI seat cap for foreign medical graduates?
Under NMC CRMI Regulations 2021 and the December 2023 notification, Indian medical colleges can allocate only up to 7.5% of their total intern seats to foreign medical graduates in old colleges. New medical colleges that do not have their own students doing CRMI can allocate 100% of seats to FMGs. This means FMGs often face waiting periods for internship seats. Start FMGE preparation early to clear quickly and secure your place.
Can I do my internship in India instead of Philippines?
No — not under FMGL 2021. The 12-month internship MUST be completed at the same foreign university where you did your academic course. After that, you do a SECOND 12-month internship (CRMI) in India. Agencies promising internship in India to save costs are misleading you and violating NMC rules.
What happens if my college is not WDOMS listed?
Your degree will be invalid for Indian practice. WDOMS listing is the first filter NMC uses. If your college is not on WDOMS, you cannot appear for FMGE, cannot register with any State Medical Council, and cannot practice in India. The March 2026 notification reinforces this by making State Medical Councils responsible for verification. Verify BEFORE paying fees at search.wdoms.org.
Do I need to clear NEET before joining BSBio in Philippines?
No. BSBio is a pre-medical course, NOT the medical degree. NEET is mandatory only before joining the MD program. Students enroll in BSBio without NEET, prepare while in Philippines, and transition to MD after qualifying. This is 100% legal because your admission to the foreign medical course (MD) happens after NEET, not the BSBio.
Will NExT replace FMGE in 2026?
NExT implementation has been deferred multiple times. As of 2026, FMGE remains the active screening test for foreign medical graduates. When NExT is implemented, it will be a common exit exam for both Indian and foreign graduates. Do not wait for NExT — start preparing for FMGE from Year 1. The syllabus overlap is nearly 100%.
What is the 10-year time limit for foreign medical graduates?
All foreign medical graduates must complete their course and internship within 10 years from the date of joining their foreign medical institution. This includes any gaps, backlogs, or repeated years. The December 2023 notification explicitly states that FMGs who exceed this limit will not be eligible for registration in India.
