# Is an MBBS Degree from the Philippines Valid in India? An In-Depth Look

A paramount concern for Indian students and their anxious parents is the legal validity and recognition of a Philippine medical degree back home. 

## The Short Answer
The short answer is yes—a Doctor of Medicine (MD) degree from a recognized Philippine university is fully recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC) and the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS). Over 15,000 Indian graduates from the Philippines have successfully cleared the FMGE and are currently practicing across India.

## Understanding NMC Gazette Regulations
To ensure smooth licensing, students must fulfill the stringent requirements outlined in the NMC Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate (FMGL) regulations:
- **Course Duration**: Minimum 54 months of medical curriculum.
- **Internship**: 12 months of supervised clinical clerkship in the host country.
- **Medium of Instruction**: English throughout the course.
- **Licensure to Practice**: Eligibility or registration to practice in the granting country.

## Preparing for FMGE and NExT
Clearing the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE)—or the upcoming National Exit Test (NExT)—is the essential milestone. Because Philippine medical colleges follow the US clinical vignette-based format, students are well-prepared for the conceptual questions featured on NExT.

## Final Takeaway
Make no mistake: while the degree is internationally valid, your individual diligence in clearing the licensing exam determines your success. Choose your college wisely and stay dedicated from day one.
