Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026.

Short version: the enquiry form does not store anything on a server. It opens WhatsApp with your message. Here is everything else.

What is collected

Nothing is collected automatically beyond what any web server logs: the pages you request, your approximate location from your IP address, and your browser type. Those logs exist at the hosting provider and are used for security and to see which pages people read.

The message form

The form on the about page is not connected to a database. When you press send, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message already written. Nothing is transmitted to this site or stored on it.

Once the message reaches WhatsApp, it is handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms, not ours. We see your name, your number and whatever you typed. That conversation is kept for as long as it is useful to your plans, and you are not added to a mailing list.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your details with colleges, recruiters or advertisers.
  • We do not run a newsletter, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from.
  • We do not ask for original documents through this site.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores one thing in your browser: your light or dark theme choice, kept in local storage. It never leaves your device and you can clear it from your browser settings at any time. There are no tracking cookies set by the site itself.

Other services this site loads

  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces. Google receives your IP address when the font loads.
  • The hosting provider, which keeps standard access logs.
  • Advertising, if ads are shown on a page, the ad network may set its own cookies under its own policy. You can control those through your browser or the network's ad settings.

Your choices

You can ask for the WhatsApp or email conversation to be deleted at any point, and it will be. If you want to know what is held about you, ask. The answer will be whatever is in our chat and nothing more.

Contact

Email contact@drnitz.com for anything to do with this policy. Privacy questions get the same treatment as everything else: a reply from me, usually within a couple of days.

Disclaimer

Last updated 25 November 2026.

This site is one doctor's experience and reading of the rules, offered in good faith. It is not a regulator, a college, or a guarantee.

Who writes this

DrDreams is written by a graduate of the Doctor of Medicine programme at Southwestern University in Cebu. It is a reference site about studying medicine in the Philippines, aimed at Indian students and their families.

The site is not affiliated with the National Medical Commission, NBEMS, the Philippine government or any college named on these pages. College names and links are there to describe the institutions, not to imply a partnership with them.

Information changes

Fees, intake dates, curricula and NMC requirements change, sometimes with little notice. Every page here carries a review date. Where rules matter to your money or your registration, check the primary source first: the NMC, WDOMS, and the college's own admissions office. Nothing on this site overrides a written communication from the college or a notice from the regulator.

No guarantee

DrDreams cannot and does not guarantee admission, a visa, an internship placement, an FMGE or NExT result, or registration with any medical council. Anyone who promises any of those outcomes in advance is overstating what any website can know.

Opinions, not rankings

The ratings and the "best for" notes are subjective judgements from five and a half years in the Philippines, plus what graduates and students reported. They are not an official ranking and they are not a measurement of teaching quality. Where the page is not first hand, it says so.

Not medical or legal advice

This site gives educational guidance about studying abroad. It is not medical advice, legal advice or immigration advice. For visa and immigration questions, use a licensed professional or the Philippine embassy in your country. For medical questions, see a doctor.

External links and errors

Links to colleges, regulators and resources are there so you can check the claims. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or their accuracy. If something here is wrong, tell us at contact@drnitz.com and it will be corrected, with the review date changed on the page.
