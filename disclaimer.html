Who writes this

DrDreams is written by a graduate of the Doctor of Medicine programme at Southwestern University in Cebu. It is a reference site about studying medicine in the Philippines, aimed at Indian students and their families.

The site is not affiliated with the National Medical Commission, NBEMS, the Philippine government or any college named on these pages. College names and links are there to describe the institutions, not to imply a partnership with them.

Information changes

Fees, intake dates, curricula and NMC requirements change, sometimes with little notice. Every page here carries a review date. Where rules matter to your money or your registration, check the primary source first: the NMC, WDOMS, and the college's own admissions office.

Nothing on this site overrides a written communication from the college or a notice from the regulator.

No guarantee

DrDreams cannot and does not guarantee admission, a visa, an internship placement, an FMGE or NExT result, or registration with any medical council. Anyone who promises any of those outcomes in advance is overstating what any website can know.

Opinions, not rankings

The ratings and the "best for" notes are subjective judgements from five and a half years in the Philippines, plus what graduates and students reported. They are not an official ranking and they are not a measurement of teaching quality. Where the page is not first hand, it says so.

Not medical or legal advice

This site gives educational guidance about studying abroad. It is not medical advice, legal advice or immigration advice. For visa and immigration questions, use a licensed professional or the Philippine embassy in your country. For medical questions, see a doctor.

What this site is

These pages explain how studying medicine in the Philippines works: the degree and how long it takes, the colleges and their recognition, the documents, the costs, the exams afterwards and the life around them. They are written to be read before money is committed, and each one carries the month it was last reviewed.

External links

Links to colleges, regulators and resources are there so you can check the claims. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or their accuracy.

Errors

If something here is wrong, tell us at drnitz@proton.me and it will be corrected, with the review date changed on the page. That offer is genuine: a wrong fee on this site costs a family more than it costs the person who wrote it.
