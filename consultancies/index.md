# Consultancies

This page will list independent consultancies that handle Philippine medical admissions and the student visa, with what they charge and who to ask for. It is empty for now.

*Last updated 25 November 2026.*

## No listings yet

There is nothing here because I have not checked any consultancy closely enough to recommend it. A list built from paid placements is worse than no list.

When a firm is added, the entry will say what it charges, which colleges it works with, who the named contact is, and whether it has handled a 9(F) visa file end to end. If a firm will not answer those questions, it will not be listed.

If you have used one and want to suggest it, send the name and what happened. Suggestions are checked, not published on trust.

**You can apply without one.**

Every college on this site has an international admissions office that deals with foreign applicants directly. The [process page](../admission-process/) runs the whole sequence, including the 9(F) visa file, and it costs nothing to follow. A consultancy is convenience, not a requirement.
