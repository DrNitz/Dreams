When a firm is added, the entry will say what it charges, which colleges it works with, who the named contact is, and whether it has handled a 9(F) visa file end to end. If a firm will not answer those questions, it will not be listed.

You can apply without one

Every college on this site has an international admissions office that deals with foreign applicants directly. The process page runs the whole sequence, including the 9(F) visa file, and it costs nothing to follow. A consultancy is convenience, not a requirement.
