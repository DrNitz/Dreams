Privacy

Short version: the enquiry form does not store anything on a server. It opens WhatsApp with your message. Here is everything else.

Last updated 22 November 2026.

What is collected

Nothing is collected automatically beyond what any web server logs: the pages you request, your approximate location from your IP address, and your browser type. Those logs exist at the hosting provider and are used for security and to see which pages people read.

The message form

The form on the contact page is not connected to a database. When you press send, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message already written. Nothing is transmitted to this site or stored on it.

Once the message reaches WhatsApp, it is handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms, not ours. We see your name, your number and whatever you typed. That conversation is kept for as long as it is useful to your plans, and you are not added to a mailing list.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your details with colleges, recruiters or advertisers.
  • We do not run a newsletter, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from.
  • We do not ask for original documents through this site.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores one thing in your browser: your light or dark theme choice, kept in local storage. It never leaves your device and you can clear it from your browser settings at any time.

There are no tracking cookies set by the site itself.

Other services this site loads

  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces. Google receives your IP address when the font loads.
  • The hosting provider, which keeps standard access logs.
  • Advertising, if ads are shown on a page, the ad network may set its own cookies under its own policy. You can control those through your browser or the network's ad settings.

Your choices

You can ask for the WhatsApp or email conversation to be deleted at any point, and it will be. If you want to know what is held about you, ask. The answer will be whatever is in our chat and nothing more.

Contact

Email drnitz@proton.me for anything to do with this policy. Privacy questions get the same treatment as everything else: a reply from me, usually within a couple of days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. There is no version history to dig through because there is nothing here worth hiding.
